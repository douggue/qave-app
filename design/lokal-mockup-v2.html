📍Lokal

Only local. No chains.

Clickable mockup, version 2. A live, Waze-style map for food trucks, pop-ups and small independent spots. Every spot and person is fictional sample data in Austin, TX, and "Lokal" is a placeholder name.

  • Illustrated map with zoom, day and night mode
  • Pull-up sheet with what's trending near you
  • Waze-style radial report button
  • Add a spot in 3 steps, with your own photo
  • Busy-times chart, truck schedules, dish ratings
  • Voice AI guide, inbox, foodie avatars, celebrations
